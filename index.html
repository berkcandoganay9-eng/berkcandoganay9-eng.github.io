About the game

AshenSoul draws on ancient mythology to bring a world of strange and powerful creatures to your hand. Open packs, grow your collection and shape a deck that fits the way you like to fight.

  • Collect over a hundred unique creature cards
  • Build and tune your own deck
  • Combine traits for powerful synergies
  • Battle through a map of increasingly tough enemies
